Where Robinhood stock tokens reopen after the weekend

Robinhood's stock tokens trade on Robinhood Chain around the clock, but their Chainlink price feeds follow US market hours. Every weekend the feed freezes, a median of 52 hours and up to 91.5 over a holiday, while the tokens keep trading in Uniswap v3 pools. Lenders such as Morpho keep valuing collateral at the frozen price until the first feed update on Sunday at 20:00 New York time.

Lull watches that gap. It is read-only: no wallet connection, no signatures, no keys.

What it shows

  • Tonight. For 33 stock tokens, the live pool price against the frozen feed, how far it has drifted, and what the last trade says about Monday's reopen: higher, lower or flat.
  • Replay. Every weekend since 1 July 2026, with the feed's Friday close, the highest, lowest and last trades with exact times, and where the feed actually reopened.
  • Positions. Paste a wallet address to see its Morpho loans backed by stock tokens, the liquidation price, and what a reopen at today's price or at the worst recent gap would do to its health.

Why it matters

  • On the weekend of 24 October, 29,713 swaps moved about $9.0M through stock-token pools while every feed was frozen.
  • About $1.71M is borrowed on Morpho against stock tokens, valued at the frozen price all weekend.
  • The largest reopen gap since July was USO at −7.75%, on the weekend of 1 August.
  • The last weekend trade called the direction of the reopen right in 286 of 341 stock-weekends since July (33 stocks, decoupled pools left out).

How it works

Feed prices come from Chainlink rounds, trades from Uniswap v3 swaps in each token's deepest USDG pool, and loans from Morpho Blue, all read directly from Robinhood Chain (chain 4663). Trade times come from block headers, never estimated. Read the method and its limits, the plain-text summary, or the source on GitHub.
