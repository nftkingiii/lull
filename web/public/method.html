How Lull calls the reopen

Robinhood stock tokens are ERC-20s on Robinhood Chain that trade around the clock. Their Chainlink price feeds follow US market hours (us_equities_24/5), so every weekend the feed stops updating while the tokens keep trading. Lull reads both sides directly from the chain and shows the gap.

1. The frozen feed

For each of the 33 equity feeds in Chainlink's Robinhood Chain directory, Lull walks the feed's rounds with getRoundData. The Friday close is the last round at or before Saturday 12:00 UTC; the reopen is the next round. Over 18 weekends since 1 July 2026 the freeze lasted a median of 52 hours, up to 91.5 over a holiday.

The expected reopen is Sunday 20:00 in New York, converted with the IANA time zone, so it is 00:00 UTC in US summer time and 01:00 UTC in winter. The real reopen is the first feed round after it, later on market holidays.

2. The weekend trades

Trades are Uniswap v3 Swap logs in each token's deepest USDG pool. Robinhood Chain's RPC returns blockTimestamp as 0 on every log, so each trade's time comes from its block header through batched eth_getBlockByNumber calls. Nothing is estimated from block numbers. Swaps with less than 100 USDG of in-range liquidity are excluded and counted.

On the weekend of 24 October 2026 that meant 29,713 swaps and about $9.0M of USDG volume while every feed was frozen.

3. The call

The call is the sign of the last weekend trade against the frozen feed: higher, lower, or flat when it sits within 0.05%. It is a direction, not a forecast of size.

Across all stock-weekends since July with swaps, the last trade's sign matched the reopen's sign in 286 of 341. Weekends where the pool stopped tracking the stock (its last price more than 10% from the reopen) are flagged as decoupled and left out.

4. The loans

Positions reads Morpho Blue directly for any wallet address. Markets are matched by Robinhood token address, never by ticker, because many unrelated tokens reuse the same symbols. For each loan Lull shows health, the liquidation price and two reopen scenarios: at today's pool price, and at the stock's worst weekend gap since July. Scenarios move the lender's oracle by the same ratio as the Chainlink feed, which it tracks within about 0.1%. About $1.71M was borrowed against stock tokens when this page was written (5 November 2026).

Limits

  • Replay uses each token's deepest pool today, because no free archive RPC exposes historical pool balances.
  • The call says nothing about how big the gap will be, and one thin pool can be wrong.
  • Lull is read-only. It never asks you to connect a wallet or sign anything, and it is information, not investment advice.

Read it yourself

The JSON API needs no key: /api/now, /api/weekends, /api/positions/{wallet}, /api/stocks. A plain-text summary is at /llms.txt and the source is on GitHub.
